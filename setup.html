Encrypt the Financials link

Your password is used to encrypt the Financials CSV link. The encrypted result is what goes into config.js — the link itself never appears in the site's source, and the app cannot fetch the financial figures until someone types the password correctly.

  1. In the sheet: File → Share → Publish to web.
  2. Publish the Financials tab, format CSV.
  3. Paste that link below, pick a password, and press Generate.

It should end in output=csv.
